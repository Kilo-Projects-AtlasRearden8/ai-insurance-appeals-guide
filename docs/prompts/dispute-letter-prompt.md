# AI Prompt: Medical Bill Dispute Letter Drafting

Use this prompt to draft a dispute letter when you believe a medical bill is
incorrect — for example, you were billed the full amount instead of your
copay, or you were billed for a service you did not receive.

## When to use this

After you have reviewed your bill and EOB side by side and identified a
discrepancy you want to dispute with the provider or insurer.

## Prompt (copy and customize)

```
I need to write a medical bill dispute letter. Here are the facts (all personal
details replaced with placeholders):

- Patient name: [Patient]
- Member ID: [XXXXXXXX]
- Claim number: [CLM-number]
- Date of service: [Month DD, YYYY]
- Provider name: [Provider]
- Billed amount: $[amount]
- Amount I was billed: $[amount]
- Amount my insurer paid (per EOB): $[amount]
- Amount I believe I owe: $[amount]
- Reason for the dispute: [brief explanation, e.g., "billed full price instead
  of negotiated rate" or "billed for a service not received"]
- Evidence I am attaching: [bullet list]

Please write a complete, professional medical bill dispute letter following
this structure:

1. Return address block (placeholders)
2. Date
3. Provider billing department name and address
4. Subject line: "RE: Bill Dispute — Account #[number] — Date of Service [date]"
5. Account number, date of service, billed amount (in the first paragraph)
6. Statement of dispute (one sentence: "I dispute this bill because...")
7. Summary of the issue (factual, 2-4 sentences)
8. Comparison table: billed amount vs. insurer-paid vs. what I owe
9. Evidence attached
10. Specific request (e.g., "correct the bill and issue a revised statement")
11. Signature line

Keep the tone factual and respectful. Do not add facts I did not provide. Use
placeholders like [Your Name] for personal details.
```
