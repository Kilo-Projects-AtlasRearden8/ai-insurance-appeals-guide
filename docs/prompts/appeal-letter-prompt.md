# AI Prompt: Appeal Letter Drafting

Use this prompt to draft the body of an insurance appeal letter. Always review
and edit AI output before sending.

## When to use this

After you have gathered your evidence and want to turn your bullet points into
a professional appeal letter.

## Prompt (copy and customize)

```
I need to write an insurance appeal letter. Here are the facts (all personal
details replaced with placeholders):

- Patient name: [Patient]
- Member ID: [XXXXXXXX]
- Claim number: [CLM-number]
- Date of service: [Month DD, YYYY]
- Denial code: [CODE] — [plain English meaning]
- Denied amount: $[amount]
- Patient responsibility: $[amount]
- Why I believe the denial was wrong: [1-2 sentences in your own words]
- Evidence I am attaching: [bullet list of documents]

Please write a complete, professional appeal letter following this structure:

1. Return address block (placeholders)
2. Date
3. Insurance company name and claims address
4. Subject line: "RE: Appeal of Claim Denial — Claim #[number] — Member #[ID]"
5. Member ID, claim number, date of service, denial code (in the first paragraph)
6. Statement of disagreement (one sentence: "I disagree with the denial...")
7. Summary of the issue (factual, 2-4 sentences)
8. Argument (why the denial was wrong, supported by the attached evidence)
9. List of enclosed documents
10. Specific request (e.g., "reprocess and reverse the denial")
11. Signature line

Keep the tone factual, respectful, and beginner-friendly. Do not add facts I
did not provide. Do not invent case law, statutes, or denial-code meanings.
Replace all personal details with placeholders like [Your Name].
```

## What to check after AI generates it

- [ ] Every `[placeholder]` is replaced with real information.
- [ ] The claim number matches your EOB exactly.
- [ ] The denial code and its meaning match your insurer's official list.
- [ ] The evidence list only includes documents you actually have.
- [ ] No emotional language or personal attacks.
- [ ] The deadline date is correct and included in your tracking (Step 4).
