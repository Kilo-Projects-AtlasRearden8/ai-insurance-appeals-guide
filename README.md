# AI Insurance Appeals Guide

A beginner-friendly manual for using AI to draft insurance denial appeals and medical bill dispute letters — step by step.

**Created by Asdale Dynamics, LLC** — founded by Dylan Asdale, a registered nurse turned AI author. This guide follows the same plain-English, step-by-step approach as *The Massive AI Manual* and the hub site [dylanasdale.github.io](https://dylanasdale.github.io).

---

## What is this guide for?

Health insurance denials and surprise medical bills are frustrating. Reading an Explanation of Benefits (EOB) can feel like decoding a foreign language. This guide shows you, step by step, how to:

1. **Read your EOB** — spot denial codes, patient responsibility, and service details.
2. **Gather evidence** — collect the medical records and supporting documents you need.
3. **Write an appeal letter** — using AI to draft clear, organized, compelling language.
4. **Track deadlines** — never miss a filing window again.

> **This is educational material only.** It is not legal advice, not medical advice, and not billing advice. Always verify information with your insurer, provider, or a qualified professional.

## Who is this for?

- Anyone who has received a denied claim or an unexpected medical bill.
- People who want to use AI as a drafting assistant (not a decision-maker).
- Beginners who want plain-language, actionable steps — no jargon without explanation.

## How to use this guide

- **Read the docs online:** Open `site/index.html` in your browser. All pages are static — no server required. You can also double-click any HTML file in the `site/` folder.
- **Read the markdown source:** Open any file in `docs/` with a markdown viewer (VS Code, Obsidian, GitHub, etc.).
- **Copy the AI prompts:** The `docs/prompts/` folder contains ready-to-use prompt templates you can paste into your favorite AI tool.
