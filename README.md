# AI Insurance Appeals Guide

A beginner-friendly manual for using AI to draft insurance denial appeals and
medical bill dispute letters — step by step.

**Created by Asdale Dynamics, LLC** — founded by Dylan Asdale, a registered
nurse turned AI author. This guide follows the same plain-English, step-by-step
approach as *The Massive AI Manual* and the hub site
[dylanasdale.github.io](https://dylanasdale.github.io).

---

## What is this guide for?

Health insurance denials and surprise medical bills are frustrating. Reading an
Explanation of Benefits (EOB) can feel like decoding a foreign language. This
guide walks you through, step by step, how to:

1. **Read your EOB** — spot denial codes, patient responsibility, and service details.
2. **Gather evidence** — collect the medical records and supporting documents you need.
3. **Write an appeal letter** — using AI to draft clear, organized language.
4. **Track deadlines** — never miss a filing window again.
5. **Dispute a medical bill** — compare your bill to your EOB and ask for a correction.
6. **Use AI safely** — treat AI as a drafting assistant, never a decision-maker.

> **This is educational material only.** It is not legal advice, not medical
> advice, and not billing advice. Always verify information with your insurer,
> provider, or a qualified professional before you act on it.

---

## Who is this for?

- Anyone who has received a denied claim or an unexpected medical bill.
- People who want to use AI as a drafting assistant — not a replacement for
  their own judgment.
- Beginners who want plain-language, actionable steps with no jargon without
  an explanation.

You don't need to be good with paperwork, good with technology, or good with
insurance. If you can open a document and type, you can use this guide.

## How to use this guide

### Option 1: Read the static site (easiest)

Open `site/index.html` in your web browser. All pages are static HTML — no
server, no installation, no internet required once you have the files.

You can also double-click any HTML file in the `site/` folder to open it
directly.

### Option 2: Read the markdown source

Open any file in `docs/` with a markdown viewer (VS Code, Obsidian, GitHub,
etc.).

### Option 3: Use the AI prompts

Copy a prompt from `docs/prompts/` (or from the "AI Prompts" page on the site),
paste it into your AI tool, and fill in the bracketed placeholders with your
own facts. Always review and edit AI output before you send anything.
